This website was created as part of my Information Visualisation Design (DECO3100) assignment at the University of Sydney.

I built this website to engage young adults in understanding how social media design can effortlessly spread and normalise fake news. It's an interactive experience where the story unfolds through data-visualisation, making the issue come to life.

I additionally performed user testing and iterated my copywriting and design to ensure the website’s message is clear and effectively communicated, while also ensuring a seamless and enjoyable user experience.
